Leadership Philosophy

Design leadership is about
multiplying your team's impact

My job isn't to be the best designer in the room — it's to create the conditions where great design happens consistently. I build clarity around vision, remove roadblocks, and trust my team's creative instincts.

Over 10+ years at Seismic, I've grown from individual contributor to leading the Financial Services and Platform design teams, mentoring designers at every stage of their careers.
