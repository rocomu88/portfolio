Leadership Philosophy

Design leadership is about
multiplying your team's impact

My job isn't to be the best designer in the room — it's to create the conditions where great design happens consistently. I build clarity around vision, remove roadblocks, and trust my team's creative instincts.

Over 12 years at Seismic, I've grown from individual contributor to leading the Financial Services design team, mentoring designers at every stage of their careers.

My Leadership Approach
Team Growth
I measure success by how much my team doesn't need me. On Governance Orchestration Service, that meant building a compliance design system and embedding seven designers directly in partner squads instead of becoming the review gate myself.

Organizational Scale
