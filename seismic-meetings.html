The Challenge

Sellers needed a tool that supported the whole meeting journey

Sales reps used meetings to engage prospects and close deals — but most tools ignored what happens after the meeting. Reviewing content, writing follow-up summaries, and identifying next steps were all fragmented, manual, and time-consuming.

Seismic fills this gap by streamlining preparation, presentation, and follow-up in a single experience. After each meeting, Aura (Seismic's AI) creates a summary with key points and decisions, tracks action items to ensure nothing is missed, suggests helpful content to share, and lets users ask questions about the meeting for quick answers. The result: sellers get better insights, stronger follow-up support, and faster sales cycles.

My Role as Design Leader

End-to-end ownership from strategy to ship

I directed the end-to-end UX design and strategy for Seismic for Meetings — a critical new feature aimed at transforming how enterprise sales teams collaborate. My role encompassed leading the discovery phase, guiding the design process, and overseeing the successful implementation of a solution that significantly boosted user engagement and efficiency.

This was one of the more complex projects in terms of AI integration. I had to lead the team in thinking not just about UI, but about how AI-generated content (summaries, action items, content suggestions) should be presented in a way that builds trust and drives adoption. That required a new design framework, not just new screens.

Discovery

Building empathy with sellers before designing for them

During the discovery phase, I helped the team build a strong foundation for the project. I led the effort to identify key user groups and worked on designing the interview process, making sure our user research included a variety of perspectives that shaped how we defined the problem.

01 · Problem Framing
Main Task
Design a sales enablement tool for meetings that seamlessly integrates preparation, presentation, and follow-up stages — empowering sellers to close more deals and achieve higher productivity throughout the seller journey.
02 · Research
Talking to Sellers
To understand seller needs, we conducted interviews with seller teams. We explored their meeting preparation process, content presentation methods, and post-meeting follow-up workflows to uncover pain points and opportunities.
03 · Synthesis
Ideation
Leveraging user insights, we conducted collaborative ideation sessions to define user flows, information architecture, and emphasis — shaping the overall UI direction and the AI integration model.
Prototyping

Testing with real sellers to validate before we built

We developed prototypes for usability testing with internal sellers. The valuable user feedback allowed us to refine the design — enhancing its functionality and user-friendliness before committing to full engineering build.

One key insight from prototyping: sellers wanted AI-generated content to be editable and controllable, not autonomous. We designed the AI outputs (summaries, action items) as starting points that sellers could quickly review and modify — building trust in the AI without creating fear of it.

Results

A new product category that opened the roadmap

Seismic for Meetings shipped and became a key differentiator in Seismic's enterprise offering. The $8M pipeline contribution validated the strategic bet on meeting intelligence, and the strong engagement metrics (5K participants/month, 2.5K content shares) proved that sellers were genuinely changing their meeting habits because of the product.

This project set the foundation for Seismic's broader investment in AI-assisted selling — establishing design patterns for AI content presentation that are now used across the platform.

$8M in current sales pipeline directly attributed to the feature — validating enterprise market demand
